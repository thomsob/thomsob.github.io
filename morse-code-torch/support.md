# Morse Code Torch Support

For help, or to report a problem, email [Support](mailto:morsetorchsupport@gmail.com). Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

## Safety

### Flashing light warning

The strobe produces a rapidly flashing light. Flashing lights can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy, and can cause discomfort, dizziness or disorientation in others.

- Don't use the strobe if you, or anyone who may see it, have epilepsy or have had a seizure.
- Don't point the light at anyone's eyes, or use it near people who are driving, cycling or operating machinery.
- Stop immediately if you or anyone nearby feels unwell.

The app's screen never flashes, only the camera light does.

### Not an emergency device

Morse Code Torch isn't a safety or emergency device, and shouldn't be relied on in an emergency. The light can stop working without warning, for example if the iPhone overheats, its battery runs low, or iOS takes the torch for another app. A Morse or SOS signal may not be seen or understood.

**In an emergency, contact the emergency services**, for example with your iPhone's Emergency SOS feature.

## Common questions

### The app says the torch is unavailable

iOS turns the torch off when the phone gets too hot, or while another app, such as the Camera, is using it. Let the phone cool down or close the other app, and the torch becomes available again.

### The light turns off when I leave the app

This is deliberate. iOS pauses apps in the background, which could leave a strobe or message stuck on, so Morse Code Torch turns the light off when you leave it.

### The fast strobe stopped after a few seconds

Without Pro, strobe speeds above 5 flashes per second run as a short preview, followed by a 30-second pause. Speeds of 5 flashes per second or less always work. Morse Code Torch Pro removes the limit.

### Tap Morse reads my dashes as dots, or splits my letters

Tap Morse reads your timing based on the speed shown under the message:

- A touch shorter than two dot lengths is a dot. A longer one is a dash.
- A pause ends the letter, and a longer pause ends the word.

If letters are being split up or misread, lower the speed with the − button. This gives you more time for each dash and each pause.

### Can I make the beam wider or narrower?

No. iOS lets apps set the brightness of the torch, but not the width of its beam.

### I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open the upgrade screen and tap **Restore Purchase**. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Restoring is also how you get Pro on a new iPhone or after reinstalling the app, at no extra cost.

### How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all purchases and refunds. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
