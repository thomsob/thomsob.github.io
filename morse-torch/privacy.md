# Morse Torch Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

Morse Torch doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.

## What the app does

- **No data collection.** The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and doesn't connect to any server of its own.
- **Settings stay on your iPhone.** The app remembers your chosen mode, brightness, speeds and Morse message so they're there next time. These are stored only on your device, and are deleted when you delete the app.
- **No photos or video.** The app uses the camera's light only. It never turns on the camera, so it can't capture images, video or sound.
- **Messages stay on your iPhone.** The Morse messages you type or tap are flashed by the light and never sent anywhere.

## Purchases

Morse Torch Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The app only receives confirmation from Apple that Pro has been bought. It never sees your name, payment details or Apple Account.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
